# customer_behavior_analysis
Data analytics project showcasing customer behavior analysis using Python, SQL, and PowerBI.

SQL Analysis (PostgreSQL)
1. Revenue by Gender
  Male customers generated $157,890, while female customers generated $75,191.

2. High‑Spending Discount Users
  Identified 839 customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount.

3. Top 5 Products by Rating
Highest‑rated items:

Gloves (3.86)

Sandals (3.84)

Boots (3.82)

Hat (3.80)

Skirt (3.78)

4. Shipping Type Comparison
Express shipping users spent slightly more on average ($60.48) than standard shipping users ($58.46).

5. Subscribers vs. Non‑Subscribers
Subscribers: 1,053 customers → $62,645 revenue

Non‑Subscribers: 2,847 customers → $170,436 revenue

6. Discount‑Dependent Products
Top items most frequently purchased with discounts:

  Hat (50%)

  Sneakers (49.66%)

  Coat (49.07%)

  Sweater (48.17%)

  Pants (47.37%)

7. Customer Segmentation
  Loyal: 3,116

  Returning: 701

  New: 83

8. Top Products per Category

9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions
Repeat buyers (>5 purchases) were more likely NOT to subscribe:

  No: 2,518

  Yes: 958

10. Revenue by Age Group
  Highest revenue: Young Adults ($62,143)  
  Lowest revenue: Seniors ($55,763)

Power BI Dashboard
Built an interactive dashboard visualizing:

  Subscription status distribution

  Revenue by category

  Sales by age group

  Shipping type preferences

  Product category performance

  Customer demographics

The dashboard ties together Python + SQL insights into a clean business‑ready interface.

Key Insights
  Non‑subscribers generate significantly more revenue due to higher volume

  Young adults are the most profitable age group

  Discount usage is extremely high for certain product types

  Express shipping correlates with slightly higher spending

  Loyal customers dominate the dataset, suggesting strong retention

Business Recommendations
  Promote subscription benefits to increase conversions

  Strengthen loyalty programs for high‑frequency buyers

  Reevaluate discount strategy for margin‑heavy products

  Highlight top‑rated items in marketing campaigns

  Target high‑value age groups with personalized offers
